The Girl Whose Name I Never Knew
10-12-2025
/images/spidey.jpeg
love, regret, honesty

Today, I saw someone. A girl with beautiful eyes, perhaps some of the most beautiful eyes I have ever seen. As a writer, I have a thousand stories to tell and a million poems to write about beauty, but somehow, I couldn't find the words to describe what I felt when I saw her.
For a moment, I wondered, What if I fall in love with her? And then came the fear. What if she leaves me someday, just like A...? Maybe it's the loneliness talking, or perhaps I'm simply afraid of getting attached to someone only to lose them again. Sometimes, the past makes us afraid of possibilities we haven't even experienced.
But do you know what the funniest thought that crossed my mind was?
Her spectacles. Hahaha! I imagined walking up to her and saying, "Excuse me, ma'am," before gently adjusting her spectacles on the bridge of her nose. Just imagine! I don't even know her name, where she's from, or who she really is. All I know is that she's my junior and belongs to my department.
The truth is, I don't have the courage to approach her and start a conversation. I can write a thousand words about love, but when it comes to expressing my own feelings, I become the quietest person in the room.
Still, if I ever get the chance, or if I somehow find the courage, I'd like to talk to her, know her, make her laugh, and perhaps, someday, fall in love with her.
Maybe we'll become something beautiful. Maybe nothing will ever happen. Who knows?
Hahaha! I'm dreaming too much, aren't I?
But what can I do? I'm a writer. Sometimes, a beautiful story begins long before we know whether it will ever become real. ❤️
