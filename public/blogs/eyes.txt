The Girl Whose Name I Never Knew
10-12-2025
/images/spidey.jpeg
love, regret, honesty

Today, I saw someone. A girl with beautiful eyes, perhaps some of the most beautiful eyes I have ever seen. As a writer, I have a thousand stories to tell and a million poems to write about beauty, but somehow, I couldn't find enough words to describe what I felt when I looked at her. Funny, isn't it? How a stranger can appear out of nowhere and make a writer want to write again.

But somewhere between admiring her beauty and imagining all the stories I could write about her, another thought crossed my mind. What if I fall in love with her, only for her to leave me someday, just like A...? And just like that, the little happiness I had found in seeing her turned into something heavier. Maybe it was loneliness talking. Maybe it was the fear of getting attached to someone only to lose them again. Or perhaps I have simply become too afraid to let anyone close enough to hurt me. I don't know.

But do you know what the funniest part of all this was? Her spectacles. Yes, her spectacles! Of all the things I could have thought about, my mind somehow decided to imagine myself walking up to her and saying, "Excuse me, ma'am," before gently adjusting her spectacles on the bridge of her nose. Hahaha! What on earth was I thinking?

The best part is that I don't even know her name. I don't know where she comes from, what makes her laugh, what kind of music she listens to, or what she dreams about when she lies awake at night. I don't know a single thing about her, except that she is my junior and belongs to my department. That's all. She's practically a stranger, and here I am, already imagining scenes that have never happened.

The truth is, I don't have the courage to walk up to her and start a conversation. I can write stories about people who confess their love, cross oceans for someone, or fight the whole world just to be together. I can give my characters the most beautiful words to express their feelings, but when it comes to my own life, I sometimes struggle to say a simple hello. Isn't that ironic? A writer who can find a thousand words for everyone else but cannot find the courage to speak a single one to the girl who caught his attention.

Still, a small part of me hopes that someday, somehow, I'll get a chance. Perhaps I'll become a little braver. Perhaps I'll stop allowing my past to decide what my future should look like. And if that day ever comes, I'd like to know her—not just admire her eyes from a distance, but discover the person behind them. I'd like to talk to her, listen to her stories, make her laugh, and share a few of my own. I'd like to know what makes her happy, what makes her sad, and all the little things that make her who she is. And who knows? Maybe, if life is kind enough, I'll get to be someone who makes her smile on an ordinary day.

Maybe we'll become friends. Maybe we'll fall in love. Maybe we'll sit together someday, laughing about something so silly that neither of us will remember why we started laughing in the first place. Or maybe none of this will ever happen. Perhaps she'll remain that beautiful girl from my department whose name I never knew. Maybe our paths will cross a few more times, and eventually, she'll become nothing more than a distant memory. And that's okay, too. I suppose not every beautiful thing we encounter in life is meant to become a part of our story. Some people arrive as chapters; others remain unanswered questions.

But for now, let me dream a little, will you? I've spent enough time being afraid of losing people I haven't even found yet. Perhaps I should give myself permission to hope for something beautiful without immediately imagining how it might end. After all, I don't know her, and I don't know what tomorrow holds. I certainly don't know whether she'll ever discover that a boy from her department has already imagined an entire universe of possibilities around a pair of beautiful eyes and a pair of spectacles.

Hahaha. I'm dreaming too much, aren't I? Maybe I am. But I'm a writer, and sometimes, before a story becomes real, it begins with nothing more than a stranger, a little loneliness, and an imagination that refuses to stay quiet.

And this time, my story begins with a girl whose name I never knew. ❤️
